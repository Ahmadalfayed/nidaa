# Store listing — English (en-US)

**App name (max 30):**
Nidaa: Offline Walkie-Talkie

**Short description (max 80):**
Walkie-talkie, chat, encrypted groups & SOS between phones with no internet

**Full description:**
Nidaa lets your phone talk directly to nearby phones — no internet, no cell coverage, no servers. Built for the desert, camping, road trips, the sea, and anywhere without signal.

🎙️ Push-to-talk walkie-talkie
Hold the button and speak; your voice reaches every nearby phone in the same group instantly.

💬 Mesh chat
Messages hop from phone to phone until they arrive, so range grows with every phone in between.

👥 Groups and channels
• Public channel: anyone nearby can join.
• Private group: join with a code and PIN. Messages and voice are encrypted (AES-256) and only members can read them, even when relayed by other phones.

🆘 SOS button
Sends your location and an emergency alert to all nearby phones, with a notification even when their screen is off.

📶 Signal meter
Measures and logs your mobile signal with location and helps you find the best spot to get coverage.

🔋 Works with the screen off
Keeps receiving and relaying messages, SOS alerts and voice while the screen is locked, with a persistent notification you can stop anytime.

📡 Works on all devices
Connects over Wi-Fi Direct, Bluetooth and hotspot, and does not require Google Play services — it works on Huawei, Samsung and others.

🔒 Privacy
No accounts, no servers. All chats and logs stay on your phone only, and you can delete them anytime.

Honestly: Nidaa does not boost your carrier signal and does not connect to regular radio walkie-talkies. It works only between phones that have Nidaa. Range between two phones is about 50–100 m in open areas.
